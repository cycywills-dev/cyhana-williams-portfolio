Cyhana Williams is a product manager and strategist with 8+ years across enterprise AI, data platforms and geospatial intelligence. She turns complex operational and spatial data into product strategy and business decisions, from AI agents used by millions of Microsoft 365 users to a distribution system that cut operating costs by about 50%.

At Microsoft, she has shaped product direction for AI agents including the Researcher agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot, People Agent and Org Explorer, running 40+ customer studies and co-creating a human evaluation governance framework that improved AI output quality by 50% and was adopted as the standard across multiple research teams.

Her geospatial analysis in the Route-to-Market project at Coca-Cola Beverages Africa, Voltic Ghana, took distribution strategy from requirements through rollout, optimizing 80,000+ customer locations and reducing operational costs by about 50% while preventing distributor territory disputes.

At Henson Geodata Technologies, she was Product Manager for GeoAI Solutions, owning delivery of IGEZA (now MyAgro360), an AI-powered pest and disease management product, and leading go-to-market planning that won the Frontier Innovation Award at the Fall Armyworm Tech Prize.

Beyond her core product work, Cyhana co-founded African Women in GIS, a 1,000+ member community across 30+ countries, and founded Unconventional Impact, a media platform built around her original GeoAI Context Intelligence framework for combining geospatial and AI thinking in unconventional fields.

Together, her work advances product strategy for AI and geospatial solutions, and advocates for inclusion in the global tech and geospatial community. She believes that diverse voices shape the future of technology.

IGEZA: Pest & Disease Control System

Henson Geodata Technologies, Product Manager, GeoAI Solutions

As Product Manager for GeoAI Solutions at Henson Geodata Technologies, I owned delivery of IGEZA (now MyAgro360), coordinating software, data and farmer research teams to deliver an AI-powered pest and disease management product that helps farmers detect crop threats, find recommended pesticides and alert agricultural extension officers.

I led go-to-market planning and represented the product in client meetings and competitions, including pitching IGEZA at the Fall Armyworm Tech Prize in Kampala as one of 20 finalists from 288 applicants.

The team won the Frontier Innovation Award (3rd place), recognized by USAID, Feed the Future and FFRAR for its potential to improve food security and protect smallholder farmers across Africa.
