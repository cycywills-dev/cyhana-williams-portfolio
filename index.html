Cyhana Williams' work has driven impact across geospatial innovation and AI technology products by turning spatial data and user insights into strategic solutions. At Microsoft, she led research projects that helped optimize Generative AI output responses (text and visual) by co-introducing a governance method for Model Output Human Evaluations, a method that helped improve AI output by more than 50%.

Her geospatial analysis in the Route-to-Market project at Coca-Cola Beverages Africa, Voltic Ghana, optimized distribution across 80,000+ customer locations, reducing operational costs by about 50% while improving service territory planning and resource allocation.

At Henson Geodata Technologies (HGT), she coordinated both software and data teams, as well as farmer-centered research, for the development of IGEZA (MyAgro360), an agricultural intelligence system that earned international recognition and won the $50,000 Frontier Innovation Award (FAW Tech Prize) for helping farmers detect crop pests and access timely support.

Beyond technical impact, Cyhana co-founded African Women in GIS, a growing network across 30+ countries with 1,000+ members that provides mentorship, training and opportunities for African women in the geospatial field.

Together, her work advances geo-business strategy, technology program management, and advocacy for inclusion in the global geospatial and tech community. She believes that diverse voices shape the future of technology.

IGEZA: Pest & Disease Control System

Henson Geodata Technologies, GeoAI products

As Project Coordinator for IGEZA at Henson Geodata Technologies, I bridged geospatial technology, user research and product strategy to deliver an innovative digital solution: an AI-powered geospatial platform that enabled smallholder farmers to detect, report and respond to Fall Armyworm outbreaks through mobile technology, location intelligence and access to expert support.

I pitched this product to several key clients, including government offices and FAW Tech Prize investors.

Following field testing and evaluation, IGEZA earned a Frontier Innovation Award, recognizing its potential to improve food security and protect smallholder farmers across Africa through technology-driven agricultural advisory services.
